How does the Referral Program works?

This program lets you earn benefits such as free recharges and validity period for your existing Smart Cut Pro product. When you refer to any person for purchase of Smart Cut Pro product, you will be directly entitle for the above mentioned offer once the referred person purchases Smart Cut Pro product.

The referral is valid until xx days, that means, your referred person must purchase the product within xx days so that you enjoy the benefit. After xx days you can refer to the same person again. You can refer to multiple persons at a time to enjoy maximum benefits.

How to Login?

  • Login by entering your product details below using your product serial number and password.
  • If you are referring for the first time or if you forgot your password, then click Dont have password to set a new password. An OTP (One Time Password) will be sent to you registered mobile number and email ID.
  • If you have registered earlier but you don't know your password, then simply click on Generate OTP, an OTP will be sent to you registered mobile number.
  • Enter OTP, enter new password, confirm new password and click on Set. (Click on "Send again", if you didn't receive OTP)
  • Once you have successfully logged in, click on Add New Referral button to proceed with referring the person.
  • Enter referral person's details with all mandatory fields and click on Submit.
